Subject: [RFC PATCH 0/3] media: rockchip: VEPU580 stateless H.264 encoder

DRAFT, not for sending: talk to Collabora (Nicolas Dufresne, Detlev
Casanova) first. They plan a Vulkan Video plus thin kernel driver path for
this block.

This series adds a V4L2 stateless encoder driver for the VEPU580 (RKVENC)
of the RK3588, on top of Paul Kocialkowski's "media: Add V4L2 H.264
stateless encode and VC8000E support" v1 (patches 1-7, generic uAPI and
v4l2-h264-enc helpers). It would be a second, non-Hantro user of that uAPI.

- The two encoder cores are independent DT nodes. They are bound as
  components of one m2m device that schedules jobs from different contexts
  on idle cores, and they share the first core's IOMMU domain.
- H.264 Baseline/Main/High, CAVLC/CABAC, I/P pictures with a single
  reference picture, and one slice.
- The kernel writes SPS/PPS through the v4l2-h264-enc RBSP writer. The
  hardware writes slice headers.
- Direct QP, CQ and CBR rate control. CBR drives the hardware's per-row
  rate control and feeds the measured average QP back to the statistics.

Register meanings and default tuning values come from Rockchip's MPP
library. They are written as named fields and formulas; there are no
register dumps.
